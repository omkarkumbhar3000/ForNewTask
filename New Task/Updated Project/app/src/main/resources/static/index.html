How it works

  1. 🧑‍🍼

    A grown-up signs up

    Add your child with a first name and date of birth. That is all we need.

  2. 👆

    Pick who is learning

    Your child never needs a password. You open their space from yours.

  3. ⭐

    Learn, play and collect

    Short lessons, a gentle quiz for each topic, and badges for great effort.

What is inside

Every course is made of short lessons. Quizzes open once the lessons are done.

    Built for little ones

    Big pictures, sounds and a "Read to me" button for early readers. The screens change with your child's age: toddlers see pictures and stars, older children see words and scores.

    Safe by design

    No adverts, no tracking and no photos of children. Children choose a friendly animal avatar and a username that never shows their real name. Only you can see your child's progress.
