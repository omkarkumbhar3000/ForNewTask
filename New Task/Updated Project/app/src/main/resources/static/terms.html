1. What CleverCubs is

CleverCubs is an early-learning website for children aged 2 to 8, used together with a parent or legal guardian. It offers picture lessons, rhymes, stories, short quizzes and badges, and shows the grown-up how their child is getting on.

2. Who may create an account

Only an adult who is the child's parent or legal guardian may create a family account and add children to it. Children do not have their own sign-in; a grown-up opens their learning space.

3. The grown-up's responsibilities

  • Keep the account password private and do not share it with the child.
  • Give accurate information, especially the child's date of birth, which sets the level of the lessons.
  • Supervise screen time in the way that suits your family.
  • Tell us if you think someone else has used the account.

4. What we can and cannot promise

CleverCubs is a learning aid. Every child learns in their own way and at their own pace. We cannot guarantee any particular learning outcome, level of progress or result, and the progress shown in the app describes activity in the app only; it is not an assessment of a child's development or ability.

We work to keep the service available and correct, but it may occasionally be unavailable or contain mistakes. Please tell us when something looks wrong.

5. Acceptable use

Please use CleverCubs only for its purpose. Do not try to access other families' information, interfere with the service, or copy its content for other purposes.

6. Content

The lessons, pictures, sounds and videos belong to CleverCubs or its content providers and are for personal, family use.

7. Ending your account

You can delete a child's data or your whole account at any time from the Account page. We may suspend an account that is misused, and we will explain why.

8. Changes

If these terms change in a way that matters, we will ask you to read and accept the new version the next time you sign in.
